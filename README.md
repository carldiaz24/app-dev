## My Favorite Series: *Forever*
**Genre:** Teen Drama, Romance 

**Released:** 2025

### Main Cast
- **Michael Cooper Jr.** as **Justin Edwards**
- **Lovie Simone** as **Keisha Clark**

#### About the series
Forever is a series that tells a story of how childhood friends, Keisha Clark and Justin Edwards reunite after some time and fall in love in high school. Rebuilding trust, boundaries, and making personal decisions are some of the hurdles they cannot overcome.

#### Why I Love It
Forever displays the truth of youthful love. The happiness, the heart-break and the lessons considered on the way. Chemistry between Keisha and Justin is genuine, and the story brings out growth and self discovery in a moving manner.

#### Reflection
The series Forever helped me to understand how the first love can influence the perception of the relationships and self-esteem of a person. Keishas and Justin story made me realize that love does not necessarily imply remaining together, but taking all the experience and learning and developing. It has helped me realize that giving up is not a failure, and at times it is a step of becoming what you are supposed to be.
